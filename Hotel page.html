Experience Bergen by bike

Transport and
parking

The airport express coach – and most other buses in Bergen – have stops by Bryggen, no more than a couple of minutes walk from the hotel. Just around the corner you will also find Rosenkrantz parking garage. If you prefer vertical travel to horizontal, there's always the Floyen Funicular just down the street.
